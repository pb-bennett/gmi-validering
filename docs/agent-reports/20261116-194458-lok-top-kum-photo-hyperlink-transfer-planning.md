## Recommendation

Start with a read-only detector and candidate-review tool in FOTO **Verktøy**, provisionally named **«Gjennomgå bildelenker på LOK/TOP…»**, and a contextual Validator action **«Gjennomgå i FOTO»** opening that same tool. Pass the exact affected source object identities and focus the review on them, while retaining access to all candidates and the full dataset. Opening either entry point stages nothing. Do not automatically select a KUM, transfer every link, or reuse the existing delivery action called `transfer`: that action means GMI → GML.

A later write stage is feasible using the existing byte patch engine, but is **not** an existing supported operation. It needs a narrowly owned hyperlink transaction integrated with current correction history, verification and delivery composition. Avoid a second editor or undo store.

**Approved decision: relocation is intentional.** LOK/TOP and KUM coordinates differ; transferring ownership moves the resulting photo position to the confirmed KUM coordinates while both objects' geometry stays unchanged. Show the original and proposed positions, their XY distance and available Z evidence, and preserve original placement provenance. Reconcile existing FOTO positions and decisions explicitly. Export and reimport must reflect the selected KUM ownership and resulting position. Recipient acceptance remains an evidence requirement before claiming Gemini VA compatibility; it does not imply that relocation needs another policy decision.

## Proposed matching and transfer contract

**Detection:** enumerate source point objects with authoritatively resolved LOK/TOP identity and image-link evidence. Show non-image, malformed, unresolved and unmatched cases with reasons; do not interpret every attachment as a photograph to move. Restrict the first write version to one live loaded GMI source and confidently associated imported assets. FOTO-only GMI evidence can support inspection, but the current coordinated export requires a live survey binding.

**Candidate matching, in priority order:**

1. Prefer an explicit parent/reference field only after its meaning is demonstrated by representative data or documentation. Resolve it within the same source to exactly one KUM; dangling, duplicate or contradictory references require review. Shared labels or equal `AnleggsID`/`S_OBJID` are supporting evidence until their uniqueness and semantics are proven, not implicit foreign keys.
2. Otherwise list nearby KUM candidates with raw identifiers, source identity, XY distance, Z/delta-Z and relevant attributes. Use a declared supported CRS and metric distance; never treat degrees as metres or use an assumed display CRS. Show all candidates inside the proposed search radius, including ties/coincident points. Radius is a review aid requiring calibration, not acceptance authority. Do not inherit Topplok's size heuristic as a write rule.
3. Treat vertical information as corroboration or a contradiction warning: inspect Z, `Høydereferanse`, `Vertikalnivå`, depth/dimensions and header `COSYSVER_EPSG` where available. Equal XY with different Z can be legitimate cover/chamber geometry. Equal Z or minimum 3D distance is not required. Missing Z, unknown vertical datum or incomparable measurement references cannot establish a match. No vertical transformation or fabricated height.

Every transfer requires explicit selection of a target and the links to move. Even a single spatial candidate remains a proposal. Missing or ambiguous candidates remain unchanged until resolved; a reliable relationship can rank a candidate but does not apply edits. Do not match across files in v1. Allow several covers/photos to one KUM only as individually reviewed choices; the presence of another candidate cannot be hidden by nearest ranking.

**Approved position behavior:** a confirmed transfer intentionally changes the photo's resulting placement from the source object's position to the selected KUM's position. Never edit LOK/TOP or KUM geometry. Preview both source and target coordinates, horizontal distance and available Z/vertical-reference evidence; where a FOTO occurrence already has an accepted alternative position, show that current position as well. Do not manufacture or transform Z. A usable target position and supported CRS are required to place the photo safely; missing or invalid evidence blocks application with an explanation. Different coordinates are expected and do not disqualify a transfer. Recipient interpretation of Z remains to be tested.

**Atomic transfer:** build one photo-owned transaction containing all selected source removals and destination additions. Resolve both cells with exact source preconditions; aggregate all additions to one destination cell before patching. Preserve destination links, order and metadata; append supported whole wrappers, preserving moved path/metadata and changing only explicitly previewed ordinal digits. Reuse extraction and encoding rules. Missing/aliased/duplicate columns, irregular objects, unsupported wrapper shapes, or unencodable resulting cells block the entire selected transaction. Do not add a column, normalize paths, rewrite geometry or serialize the display model.

Prevent new duplicates using full reference/asset evidence, never basename alone. If KUM already refers to the same confidently resolved asset, show **«Finnes allerede på KUM»**: preserve its existing wrapper and require explicit approval of source-only removal. Conflicting metadata, differing paths of uncertain equivalence, and case/Unicode collisions require review. Existing duplicates elsewhere are preserved. Repeated source references to one asset follow the existing complete edge grouping in v1, visibly showing the count; independent duplicate-member selection would require an explicit contract extension. Other links on the source, shared references on other objects, and non-image members stay untouched.

Extend the existing registered history with a narrowly validated photo-owned transaction type; keep ordinary field editing's reserved-column rejection. Reuse undo/redo/discard, exact dry-run approval, source checks and proposed-output verification. This requires deliberate changes to ownership checks and compilation, not a flag that bypasses them. Add an independent transfer verifier: selected removals plus approved destination additions/duplicate reuse, retained references and metadata unchanged, no unintended affected objects. The current removal verifier expressly disallows additions and cannot certify a transfer.

Compose field corrections, these ownership transfers, and export-time GML removals **once against the original**. Reject incompatible operations on the same occurrence/cell until reviewed; do not sequentially patch output or let the last operation win. Revalidate target/source Tema and relationship evidence after relevant edits or history changes. Later transactions depending on a discarded transfer must become blocked or be rebuilt explicitly.

Preserve unrelated delivery decisions. The approved KUM relocation is intentional and must not itself trigger the ordinary move-to-GML default.

Publish association, current position, inspector, counts and marker changes together so FOTO visibly shows the selected KUM and its position. Invalidate delivery snapshots/plans, download readiness and attachment accounting on stage/undo/redo/discard. Recompute Validator against the actual verified proposed GMI after each transition, including undo/redo/discard; display the resulting findings and remaining links rather than suppressing a diagnostic. All GMI-producing export modes must include the staged ownership transfer or block explicitly; split delivery must not silently omit it. After revised GMI export and normal new-revision reimport, FOTO must associate the photo with KUM and derive its position from that KUM. ## Decisions and evidence needed

- Confirm the precise recovery flow for missing photographs and unavailable bindings; the approved behavior is to explain them and retain read-only review where source evidence is available. Recommendation: require confident assets for writes.
- Supply/confirm any actual relationship-field semantics and whether a cover can legitimately correspond to several chambers. No relationship schema or numerical search radius is approved by this investigation.
- Decide how to present available Z and vertical-reference uncertainty and record recipient handling of Z; FOTO's current placement is horizontal. The intentional XY relocation and both entry points are approved decisions.
- Approve complete per-object/asset reference grouping and explicit duplicate reuse, plus the detailed conflict-resolution interaction for existing accepted positions and GML/Ikke choices.
- Confirm whether limited support with explicit blockers is acceptable for bare/mixed/unsupported grammar and absent hyperlink columns. Recommendation: do not widen parsing or schema editing for v1.

Real GMI samples are **not necessary to build synthetic read-only detection**, but representative authorized, read-only examples are needed before claiming reliable automatic relationship evidence or calibrating proximity criteria. Obtain LOK/TOP/KUM combinations with identifiers, heights, multiple candidates, shared links and populated KUM links; keep private content out of tracked fixtures/reports.

Before writes ship, obtain recipient evidence for the exact Gemini VA import path/version: controlled original versus transferred GMI with unchanged physical photo bytes and object geometry, source removal, destination opening, existing/shared links, wrapper ordinals, duplicates and the intentionally changed position at KUM. User/domain review must confirm which photographs belong to the KUM. The existing validator assertion and unrelated GML success do not establish this. Without samples/evidence, remain at read-only review or explicitly experimental planning; do not present nearest matching or compatibility as proven.
